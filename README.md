# World Tools

A Sandboxels mod for an empty beach garden. You place every wall, nest, and plant yourself. There is no prebuilt map.

World Tools adds gravity controls, a straight ruler, cooking, farming, white-and-brown room pieces, and companions who learn, choose some jobs on their own, and write notes to each other.

## Files

- `world_tools.js` — the mod
- `plant_handbook.pdf` — full plant-style guide
- `handbook.txt` — plain-text copy of the guide

## Load it

1. Upload `world_tools.js` somewhere with a direct link, such as a public GitHub Gist, and copy the Raw URL.
2. Open [neal.fun/sandboxels](https://neal.fun/sandboxels).
3. Click **Mods**.
4. Paste the full URL ending in `.js`, press Enter, and refresh.

Click **Recipe Book** in the game to turn short help pages. Open `plant_handbook.pdf` for the full guide.

## What you can do

- Change gravity: down, up, left, right, or off, and make it stronger or weaker.
- Draw a straight wall with **Straight Ruler**. Click a start pixel, then an end pixel.
- Cook from wheat to bread, pizza, pie, soup, and drinks. **Cook Tool** cooks almost any raw food.
- Farm wheat, tomato, carrot, potato, lettuce, corn, and berry. Water the soil, plant a seed, then harvest when the crop color is ripe.
- Place free nests, beach sand, palms, and white or brown furniture anywhere.
- Give a companion a job. After the order fades, they may choose for themselves. Hunger sends them to food. Night sends them to rest.
- Let companions write to a nearby friend. The friend can learn the note. **Read Note** shows the words they know.

## Ranks

Companions start as **new**, then become **learning**, **practiced**, and **skilled**. Practice makes them move more often and notice food and furniture from farther away.
